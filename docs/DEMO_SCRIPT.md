# 90-second walkthrough

1. **Purpose — 15 seconds**  
   “Who Gets Summarized?” helps editors inspect what disappears when a long
   public record becomes a short policy brief.

2. **Source — 15 seconds**  
   The case uses the official Hong Kong Legislative Council record of an oral
   question on AI development from 31 January 2024: 14 substantive turns, 7
   speakers and 3,463 words, each retained with page-level provenance.

3. **Interaction — 25 seconds**  
   Switch between “Balanced public record,” “Policy and safeguards,” and
   “Implementation and access.” Show how selected turns change while omitted
   turns remain visible in the ledger.

4. **Audit — 20 seconds**  
   Point to speaker coverage, topic coverage, retained word share and the
   representation-gap score. Then show the 200-run stability chart.

5. **Code and limitation — 15 seconds**  
   The selection engine, source record and tests are included. The system does
   not infer truth; it exposes editorial trade-offs and returns readers to the
   official record.
